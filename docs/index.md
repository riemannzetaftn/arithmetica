# Arithmetica: A Blog Dedicated to Number Theory

Welcome to *Arithmetica*.

## About the Author

I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Mathematics at KAIST, South Korea. My academic interests lie broadly in number theory, with a particular emphasis on analytic number theory. I enjoy reinterpreting existing theories and filling in the details in standard literature.

## About the Contents

This blog is a record of my studies. The contents include background in different areas of mathematics, which will in turn be used to understand the most profound results in number theory.
